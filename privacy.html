Privacy Notice

The ChiEAC Talent Directory is designed to help connect ChiEAC members with employers, organizations, and professional opportunities. Member information displayed in the directory is intended for professional networking and recruitment purposes.

Information Displayed

Member profiles may include professional information such as name, professional title, biography, education, skills, certifications, projects, professional interests, location, professional contact information, LinkedIn profile, resume, and profile photo.

Consent and Public Display

Member information is displayed in the ChiEAC Talent Directory only after the member has provided permission for public display and the profile has been approved for publication.

Members should provide only information that they are comfortable making publicly available. Information submitted for a profile may be reviewed before it is published in the directory.

Corrections, Updates, and Removal

Members may request corrections or updates to their profile information if their professional information changes.

Members may also request that their profile be removed from the public directory. Approved changes or removal requests will be reflected in the directory after they have been reviewed and processed.

Use of Information

Information in the ChiEAC Talent Directory is intended to support professional networking, recruitment, collaboration, and career development opportunities.

The directory should not be used to collect or publish sensitive personal information such as home addresses, identification numbers, immigration documents, or private employment records.

Members who would like to request a profile correction, update, or removal can use the ChiEAC Talent Directory profile form.
