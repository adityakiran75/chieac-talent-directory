About ChiEAC Talent Directory

The ChiEAC Talent Directory provides a centralized location where members can showcase their professional experiences, education, skills, projects and career interests.

The directory helps recruiters, employers and partner organizations discover qualified ChiEAC talent and connect with professionals from diverse backgrounds.

Why use this directory?

The ChiEAC Talent Directory helps connect talented professionals with organizations seeking skilled and diverse candidates.

  • Discover professional skills and experience.
  • Connect with qualified ChiEAC members.
  • Explore education, projects, and career interests.
